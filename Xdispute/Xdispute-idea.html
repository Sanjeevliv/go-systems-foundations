XDispute: The AI Referee for Internet Arguments

A simple, fun tool that settles online debates with fast fact-checking and instant, shareable scorecards.

What is it? AI Debate Referee • Who uses it? People arguing on Twitter, Reddit & WhatsApp • Build Type: Solo Weekend Project

The Problem Everyone Has Seen

We’ve all seen it: two people arguing endlessly in Twitter replies, Reddit comments, or WhatsApp groups. They argue about movie box office numbers, sports referee calls, or phone specs.

Nobody ever concedes. They start throwing personal insults, changing the subject, or making up numbers. Why? Because there is no neutral referee in the room to say who is actually right.

Enter XDispute: You tag the bot or upload a screenshot, and within 2 seconds an AI referee reads both sides, fact-checks the claims, and produces a neat, official-looking Verdict Card.

Three Referee Personalities: From Polite to Savage

Debates have different vibes. A technical disagreement on LinkedIn needs a different tone than a fiery sports argument on Twitter. Users can pick from 3 referee modes:

👔

1. Professional Mode

Clean, formal, and strictly objective. Uses academic framing, highlights verified citations, and avoids all sarcasm. Perfect for workplace Slack/Teams channels, LinkedIn, and technical disagreements.

⚖️

2. Moderate Mode (Default)

Balanced, conversational, and fair. Explains who won in simple everyday terms, points out logical fallacies without being insulting, and delivers a quick, friendly resolution for general debates.

🔥

3. Strong Mode (Savage Roast 🔥)

Brutally honest, hilarious, and completely merciless. Beyond scoring the facts, the AI unleashes an unhinged, personalized roast dissecting the loser's weak excuses, fake receipts, and timeline meltdown. Built for maximum viral laughter on Twitter and group chats where someone needs to get cooked!

How the AI Judges Who Won

The AI follows 3 simple, fair rules so nobody can complain about favoritism:

🔍

1. Real Facts First

Did you cite real facts and dates, or did you just make things up? Genuine evidence earns the most points.

🧠

2. Does It Make Sense?

Did you actually answer their point, or did you dodge the question and try to change the topic?

🚫

3. No Personal Insults

If you start calling the other person names instead of debating the topic, you immediately lose points.
